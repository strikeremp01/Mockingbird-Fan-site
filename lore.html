Faction Lore

The Art of the Heist

Operating within the high-tech shadows of New Eridu, Mockingbird is an elusive phantom thief syndicate. Unbound by municipal laws, they adhere strictly to a code of theatrical justice—targeting corrupt elites, corporate monopolies, and shadow figures who manipulate society from behind closed doors.

Public Facade & Underground Ops

To high society, Mockingbird members maintain immaculate public identities as art collectors, socialites, and aristocrats. Beneath the surface, they execute high-stakes Hollow retrievals and covert infiltrations, staying steps ahead of Public Security and rival factions.

The Phantom Philosophy

Every mission executed by Mockingbird is treated not merely as a heist, but as a masterpiece. Leaving behind distinct signatures and calculated outcomes, they dismantle corrupt infrastructure while exposing secrets that the powerful work tirelessly to hide.
